# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in NanoExtend harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for different experience levels, accepting responsibility, and prioritizing community well-being.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information, or conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers' contact method. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy, and apply proportionate consequences, including warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent bans.
